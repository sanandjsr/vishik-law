About VIDHIK Law Firm

A Pradhhaan's Legal Initiative

VIDHIK Law Firm is a full-service legal practice headquartered in Greater Noida, Gautam Buddh Nagar, serving individuals, families, startups, corporations, and financial institutions across Delhi NCR and Pan India. Founded on the enduring principles of Dharma, ethical advocacy, and thorough preparation, our firm bridges the gap between traditional courtroom litigation, specialized tribunal practice, and modern corporate advisory.

Legal challenges require both statutory precision and strategic foresight. Whether navigating high-stakes debt recovery proceedings before DRTs/DRATs, resolving real estate disputes under RERA, defending against cyber or criminal allegations, or handling sensitive matrimonial matters, we approach every brief with meticulous research and unwavering integrity.

Founding Partners

YP

Advocate Yogendra Pradhhaan

Founder & Managing Partner · Alumnus, Symbiosis Law School, Pune · Member, Delhi High Court

Advocate Yogendra Pradhhaan leads VIDHIK Law Firm with a focus on strategic litigation, banking and debt recovery disputes, and corporate advisory. An alumnus of the prestigious Symbiosis Law School (Symbiosis Law College), Pune, he combines strong academic grounding with extensive courtroom experience across Gautam Buddh Nagar, Delhi NCR, and Pan India. As an active member of the Delhi High Court, he regularly represents financial institutions, corporate entities, and individual clients before the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi, District Courts, Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRTs), Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunals (DRATs), RERA authorities, and NCLT benches nationwide.

Core Focus Areas: Banking, Finance & Pan-India DRT/DRAT Litigation; Corporate, Commercial & Arbitration Disputes; Civil & RERA Litigation; Intellectual Property Rights (IPR).
KP

Advocate Kavitta B. Pradhhaan

Founder Member & Partner · Head of Criminal Defense, Cyber Crime & Family Law

Advocate Kavitta B. Pradhhaan is a Founder Member and Partner heading the Criminal Defense, Cyber Crime, and Family & Matrimonial Law divisions at VIDHIK Law Firm. Known for her composed courtroom advocacy and empathetic client counsel, she guides individuals and families through sensitive, high-stakes personal and criminal proceedings across Gautam Buddh Nagar, Delhi NCR, and appellate forums.

Core Focus Areas: Criminal Defense, Anticipatory/Regular Bail & Quashing Petitions; Cyber Crime & IT Act Proceedings; Section 138 NI Act (Cheque Dishonor) & White-Collar Offenses; Divorce, Child Custody, Maintenance, Alimony & Domestic Violence Matters.
